About the Experiment

There is no shortage of people online promising easy money. Digital Income Experiment exists to cut through that. We try the methods ourselves and report back on what actually happens.

What we do

We test one way of earning online at a time, starting with affiliate marketing. We share regular updates on what's working, what needs work and what we struggled with, and when a process works we write it up as a step-by-step guide.

What we don't do

  • We don't publish income figures or screenshots.
  • We don't promise that anything will make you money.
  • We don't recommend products without telling you whether we've used them.

Who's behind it

Digital Income Experiment is run by one person in the UK, alongside a full-time job. That means the methods we test have to fit around real life, which is probably true for you too.
