Pure Pet Food

Gently dried natural dog food. Just add water, stir and serve.

What it is

Pure Pet Food makes gently dried dog food from whole ingredients. You add water, stir, leave it for a few minutes and serve. It is delivered direct to your door on a flexible subscription, with a range of recipes to choose from.

Who it's for

Dog owners who want a natural-ingredient food that is easier to store and prepare than fresh or raw feeding.

Our experience

We feed Pure Pet Food to our own dog regularly. Our dog is keen on it, and the add-water preparation takes a couple of minutes.

Honest downsides

  • It costs more than standard dry kibble.
  • It is subscription-based, so keep an eye on delivery dates and amend or pause as needed.
  • Switching food should be done gradually, and it is worth checking with your vet if your dog has a sensitive stomach or a health condition.
