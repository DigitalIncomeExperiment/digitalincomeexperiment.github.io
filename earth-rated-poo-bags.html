What it is

Earth Rated poo bags are thicker than the thin rolls you find in most supermarkets, with easy-tear perforations so a bag comes away cleanly first time. They come as refill rolls or boxed, in scented and unscented versions.

Who it's for

Any dog owner who is fed up with bags that tear, leak or will not separate from the roll halfway through a walk.

Our experience

We use these on our own dog walks every day. The difference from cheap thin bags is noticeable: no tears, no leaks and no fumbling with the roll.

Honest downsides

  • They cost more per bag than budget supermarket rolls.
  • They are still plastic, so they are not a zero-waste option.
  • Some people find the scented version too strong, so pick unscented if that matters to you.
