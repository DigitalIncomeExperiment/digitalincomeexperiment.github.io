What it is

Where is Maverick is a rhyming hide-and-seek picture book for children aged 2 to 6. Children follow the clues to find Maverick, a playful cockapoo, hiding around the house. It is available in paperback and on Kindle.

Who it's for

Parents, grandparents and gift-buyers looking for a fun, affordable book for young children, especially dog lovers.

Our experience

The rhyme and the hide-and-seek element make it a good read-aloud book, and children enjoy spotting Maverick on each page.

Honest downsides

  • It is a short book, best suited to younger children.
  • If your child is already reading independently, they may outgrow it quickly.
