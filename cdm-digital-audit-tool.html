What it is

The CDM Digital Audit Tool is a structured digital checklist for carrying out CDM 2015 compliance audits on site. It replaces paper forms, scattered spreadsheets and site notes with one simple tool that needs no training to use.

Who it's for

Site managers, principal contractors, project managers and anyone responsible for CDM 2015 compliance on UK construction sites.

Our experience

The tool was built from real UK site experience, with the aim of making CDM site audits quicker and more consistent.

Honest downsides

  • It supports your CDM process but does not replace competent judgement or your company's own procedures.
  • It is built around UK CDM 2015, so it is not designed for other countries' regulations.
