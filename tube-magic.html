What it is

Tube Magic is an AI-powered tool for YouTube creators. It helps with researching video ideas and drafting scripts, from the opening hook through to the outro.

Who it's for

YouTube creators, including faceless channels, who find the planning and scripting stage slows them down.

Our experience

We have not used Tube Magic ourselves yet. As with any AI writing tool, expect to edit what it produces.

Honest downsides
