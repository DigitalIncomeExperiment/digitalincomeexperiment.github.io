What it is

ProDen PlaqueOff is a seaweed-based powder you add to your dog's food once a day. It is designed to be an easy addition to a dental care routine, particularly for dogs that will not tolerate brushing.

Who it's for

Owners who struggle to brush their dog's teeth and want a simple daily add-on to their routine.

Our experience

We use PlaqueOff regularly with our own dog. It is easy to add to meals, and our dog does not notice it in his food.

Honest downsides

  • It works gradually, so it is not a quick fix.
  • It does not replace vet dental checks or professional cleaning when needed.
  • It contains seaweed, which is naturally high in iodine. Check with your vet first if your dog has a thyroid condition.
