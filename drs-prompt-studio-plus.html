What it is

DRS Prompt Studio Plus is a digital prompt library. It gives you ready-made prompts you can copy into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or similar tools, organised by task, so you are not starting from a blank box each time.

Who it's for

People who use AI tools regularly but find writing good prompts slows them down.

Our experience

It works with whichever AI tools you already use, as it is a set of prompts rather than software.

Honest downsides

  • You still need access to an AI tool to use the prompts.
  • Prompts give you a strong starting point, but results still need checking and editing.
