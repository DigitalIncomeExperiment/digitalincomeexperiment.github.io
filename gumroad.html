Gumroad

An established platform for selling digital products, with a marketplace for discovery.

What it is

Gumroad is a long-running platform for selling digital products, courses and memberships. There is no monthly fee. Gumroad takes a percentage of each sale, and it has a marketplace where buyers can discover products.

Who it's for

Creators who want a second sales channel, or who value the chance of being found through a marketplace.

Our experience

We have tried Gumroad as a second channel alongside Payhip.

Honest downsides

  • Its per-sale fees are higher than some alternatives.
  • Marketplace discovery is not guaranteed, so most sales still come from your own promotion.
