Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

This page explains what information is collected when you use this site.

Information we collect

This site has no accounts, contact forms or newsletter sign-up, and we do not set our own cookies or run analytics tracking. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we don't share them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads.

Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link and leave this site, the retailer or affiliate network (for example Amazon or Digistore24) may set cookies to track the referral. Their own privacy policies apply once you are on their site.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have rights over your personal data. If you have a question about privacy, contact us. You can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we add analytics, a newsletter or anything else that collects data, we will update this page first.

We try the ways people say you can earn online and report back honestly on what works. We never publish income figures or promise results.
