What it is

A ready-made budget planner that runs in Google Sheets. You log your income and spending, and the sheet builds monthly summaries, spending breakdowns and savings goal tracking for you.

Who it's for

People who like the idea of a spreadsheet budget but do not want to build one from scratch, and who are comfortable using Google Sheets.
