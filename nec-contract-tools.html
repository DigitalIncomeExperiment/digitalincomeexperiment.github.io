What it is

A range of practical tools for administering NEC contracts, including an Early Warning Notice template, a Compensation Event template covering all four stages, a browser-based contract manager and a site notification form that works offline. They are sold individually or as bundles.

Who it's for

Project managers, contract administrators, supervisors and site teams working on NEC contracts in UK construction and infrastructure.

Our experience

The tools are built around how NEC contracts are actually administered day to day, from notification through to assessment.
