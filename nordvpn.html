What it is

NordVPN is a virtual private network (VPN) service. It encrypts your internet traffic and routes it through its own servers, which helps protect your connection on public Wi-Fi in cafés, hotels and airports.

Who it's for

People who often use public Wi-Fi, travel, or simply want an extra layer of privacy online.

Our experience

We have not reviewed NordVPN in depth. Check the current plans and the money-back terms on their site before you buy.

Honest downsides

  • Longer plans are cheapest per month but are paid up front.
  • A VPN can slow your connection slightly.
  • Some streaming and banking sites block or question VPN connections.
