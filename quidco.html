What it is

Quidco is a UK cashback site that works the same way as TopCashback: start your shopping from Quidco and a percentage of your spend is tracked back to you.

Who it's for

UK online shoppers who want to compare cashback rates before they buy.

Our experience

We use Quidco alongside TopCashback. Neither wins every time, which is why we check both before a big purchase.

Honest downsides

  • Payouts can take weeks or months to confirm.
  • Tracking is not guaranteed, so keep your receipts.
  • Juggling two cashback sites adds a step to every purchase.
